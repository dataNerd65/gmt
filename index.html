Our Story

Gods Mercies Ministries Tabernacle began as a small prayer gathering in Nairobi, a handful of families who believed that God’s mercy is new every morning and meant to be shared.

What started in a living room has grown into a church family that worships, studies and serves together. Through every season of growth, our heart has stayed the same: to welcome people as they are, and to walk with them toward who God made them to be.

Today we gather every week to worship, and we spend the rest of the week caring for our neighbours through prayer, counselling and practical help.

Beliefs & Vision

What we hold to, and where we believe God is leading us.

Our Vision

To see Nairobi transformed by the mercy of God, with families restored, young people discipled and every neighbourhood touched by the love of Christ.

Our Mission

To welcome all people, teach the whole Bible, pray without ceasing and serve our community with practical compassion.

What We Believe

The Bible
The Scriptures are God’s inspired word and our final authority for faith and life.
One God
One God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
Jesus Christ
Fully God and fully man, who died for our sins and rose again.
Salvation by grace
We are saved by grace through faith, not by our own works.
The Holy Spirit
He empowers believers to live holy lives and to serve the church.
The Church
A community of believers called to worship, fellowship and mission.
